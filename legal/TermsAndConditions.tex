\section{AI-generated output — accuracy and limitations}

The App uses a monocular depth-estimation model to infer three-dimensional structure
from a single flat image. This is, by its nature, an \emph{estimation} problem: the true
depth of a scene cannot be determined from one picture, and the model produces a
plausible interpretation rather than a measurement.

You should therefore expect, and accept, that:

\begin{itemize}
  \item output may be inaccurate, distorted, inconsistent between runs, or simply
        unlike what you expected;
  \item results depend heavily on input quality — lighting, contrast, focus,
        occlusion, reflective or transparent subjects, and unusual compositions all
        degrade the estimate;
  \item dimensions, proportions, thicknesses and relative depths in an exported model
        are \textbf{not to scale and are not metrologically valid}; and
  \item the App is a creative and educational tool. It must not be relied upon for
        engineering, architectural, medical, forensic, navigational, structural or any
        other purpose where dimensional accuracy affects safety, health, legal rights
        or property.
\end{itemize}
